Training dynamics and the occupancy scan. (a,b) For each cell, the QSP run whose hitting time is closest to the median (or the first run when all hitting times are censored), with its matched scalar and frozen-kernel references. Time is the gradient-descent step multiplied by the step size. The gray Lower bound exp[-2(d+1)t] is the continuous-gradient-flow bound on normalized loss, shown as a reference for the discrete gradient-descent trajectories; it is not asserted as a bound for the discrete iterates. Each upper inset shows that cell's per-run scalar/QSP and frozen-kernel/QSP convergence-time ratios on a logarithmic scale. Boxes show the quartiles and median, whiskers span the retained minimum and maximum, and points are individual paired runs. Labels give valid pairs/total runs; each reference uses its own finite, positive pairs, so the distributions are conditional on uncensored QSP/reference pairs. Frozen-kernel crossing times come from the analytic reference and may exceed the observation cap used for QSP gradient descent. The loss axes extend above unity to accommodate the insets. (c) Median scalar-closure error epsilon versus occupancy w=n/sqrt(d), with interquartile error bars. Each seed's maximum over sampled times through tau=T=2 is taken before the ensemble summary. The horizontal dotted line marks epsilon=0.1 as a visual reference. The inset compares fixed-w sequences with sqrt(n)(1+ln n) shape references, each anchored at its smallest n and restricted to that sequence's n range; ln is the natural logarithm. (d) Final/initial RMS target error on the between-node test grid at tau=T=2, again with medians and interquartile ranges. R_test measures an error norm ratio, not output-vector displacement.
